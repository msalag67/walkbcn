            Your data & privacy

            WalkBCN learns from how you use it, so it can pick the stories you enjoy. It remembers the interests you pick, the stories you play and finish, and your 👍/👎.

            All of this stays in this browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and there is no account.

            To draw your route along the streets, the points of the route (including your start, if you chose one) are sent to the OpenStreetMap walking router. Address searches send only the text you type.

            Learning only changes the order and choice of stories already in the app. It never writes or publishes new ones, and it must never put engagement or commercial interests above historical accuracy, your privacy or respect for local residents.
