# WalkBCN — Scope (updated 25 October 2026)

## What the MVP does

WalkBCN is a personalised virtual travel guide that helps tourists discover Barcelona through tailored walking routes and audio stories.

- Users select their interests.
- The app creates a personalised route with 4–6 stops.
- Users follow the route through an interactive map.
- Each stop provides a short, engaging audio story, **told from the angle of the interests the user picked** (added after Session 6).
- **Activities (added after Session 6):** users pick Restaurants or Nightlife and their preferences, and get a hand-picked list of places. "See & book" opens the place on Google Maps. This exists to test demand; there is no booking inside the app.

## What it deliberately does NOT do

For the MVP, we will not include:

- AI chatbot or voice assistant
- Architecture/history scanner
- Gamification
- Forum or community
- Concert and **event** recommendations (Activities lists venues, not specific events or dates)
- Live availability checks, reservations or ticket purchases made by the app or by AI
- Affiliate links
- User accounts or payments
- Coverage of the whole city

**Rule for adding later:** We only add a feature if user testing shows that it solves a real user need or provides significant value.

**Change log:** After Session 6 we moved "personalised story versions" and a limited "Activities" list into the MVP. Activities was not backed by user testing, which breaks our own rule above; we keep it only as a demand test (we will measure clicks on "See & book") and will remove it if users do not use it.

## Main assumption tested

**Tourists want personalised guidance adapted to their interests rather than a generic travel guide.**
