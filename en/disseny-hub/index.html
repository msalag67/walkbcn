Disseny Hub (Museu del Disseny)

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

Look at this dark building that seems to hang in the air, with a huge overhang reaching out over the square. People nicknamed it the stapler, and once you see it from the side you will understand why. It is the Disseny Hub, designed by the studio MBM, founded by the architects Josep Martorell, Oriol Bohigas and David Mackay, and built between 2008 and 2013. Its outside is covered in zinc and large windows, and part of the building sits below the level of the square. Since 2014 it has been home to the Museu del Disseny, Barcelona's design museum. Inside, it brings together the collections of four older museums that used to be scattered across the city: decorative arts, ceramics, textiles and fashion, and graphic arts, more than seventy thousand objects, from chairs and dresses to posters. Now look around you. In Ildefons Cerdà's plan for the Eixample, Glòries was imagined as the new centre of the city. For decades it became instead a huge road junction, with traffic flying overhead. Little by little it is being turned into a park and a meeting point between the Eixample, Poblenou and the Clot, and the tall rounded tower beside you, the Torre Glòries, has become one of its landmarks.

Tip: The square outside is a public space; the museum has a ticket and opening hours. Keep cycle lanes and the park paths clear.
Enjoy it from outside
What to look at
The huge overhang over the square (the 'stapler'), the zinc and glass skin, and the Torre Glòries beside it.
Where to listen
On the square in front of the entrance, out of the cycle lane.
Visiting
The outside and the square are free. The museum has a ticket and opening hours: check its website.
