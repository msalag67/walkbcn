Neighbourhood: Gràcia

Plaça del Sol

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

Welcome to Plaça del Sol, the Square of the Sun. For generations, this has been one of the places where Gràcia's neighbours come together. It helps to know that Gràcia was once a separate town, and it only became part of Barcelona in 1897. You can still feel that village spirit in the way people use squares like this one. Here, squares are the neighbourhood's living rooms, places to meet friends, chat and watch the evening go by. Take a moment to look at the open sky above you. That sunshine is what gives the square its character, and its name. After work, people drift in to sit on the terraces with a drink and let the day wind down. Now here's something you'd never guess from all that brightness. During the Spanish Civil War, Barcelona was bombed from the air, and a shelter was dug beneath this square. Imagine neighbours hurrying underground to take cover, right below where you're standing. Barcelona's residents built many shelters like this across the city to protect themselves. Look down at the ground for a moment and let that sink in, then look back up at the life around you. Finally, look at the balconies that frame the square on every side. Behind them are real homes, with families who have to get up in the morning. So enjoy the square the way locals do, relaxed and unhurried, and keep your voice low as the evening goes on. That's how this living room stays welcoming for everyone.

Tip: Residents live right above the terraces: keep voices down late at night.

What to look at
The balconies all around: these are homes. The square has a Civil War shelter beneath it, not visible from above.
Where to listen
Before you enter the square, at the corner of Carrer de Maspons, rather than under the balconies or between the terrace tables.
