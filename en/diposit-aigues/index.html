Dipòsit de les Aigües

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

Look at this huge brick building with a tower at each corner. It was built between 1874 and 1880 as a water tank, designed by Josep Fontserè, the architect of the Ciutadella park across the street, to feed the park's great cascade and water its gardens. The water was held up on top, and underneath a forest of brick pillars and arches carried that enormous weight. A young architecture student called Antoni Gaudí is said to have helped with the calculations. The tank did not stay a tank for long. From 1896 to 1977 the building was a municipal shelter for poor people: over the entrance on this street you can still read the old words Asilo Municipal del Parque. Then it became a store for the fire brigade, a garage for police vehicles and a judicial archive. In the late 1980s the architects Lluís Clotet and Ignacio Paricio were asked to rehabilitate it, the Universitat Pompeu Fabra bought it in 1992, and in 1999 it opened as the university's library. They kept the brick pillars and arches and placed the reading floors between them. In 2024 it was declared a cultural asset of national interest. Today it is a place to study, so enjoy it from outside.

Tip: It is a university library: enjoy it from the street and keep the entrance clear for students.

What to look at
The four corner towers and the arches of the façade; over the entrance on Carrer de Wellington, the old inscription 'Asilo Municipal del Parque'.
Where to listen
On the park side of Carrer de Wellington, opposite the building, not at the library entrance.
Visiting
A library for the university community: the building is meant to be enjoyed from outside. Occasional open days.
