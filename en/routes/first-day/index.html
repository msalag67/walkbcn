  • Santa Maria del Mar · El Born
    This is the cathedral of the people. It was built in just 54 years by the dock workers and fishermen of the neighbourhood.

    The cathedral took over 600 years to finish — the main façade was only completed in the late 1800s.

  • Plaça de Sant Felip Neri · Gothic Quarter
    This is one of the quietest corners of the old town, and one of the saddest. Look at the church façade: it is pitted with holes.
  • Temple d'August · Gothic Quarter
    Hidden inside a small courtyard on Carrer del Paradís stand four Roman columns, nearly two thousand years old.
  • Plaça Reial · Gothic Quarter
    This elegant arcaded square was built in the 19th century on land where a convent once stood.
  • Gran Teatre del Liceu · La Rambla
    Behind this modest façade on the Rambla hides one of Europe's great opera houses, opened in 1847.

  • Palau de la Música Catalana · Sant Pere
    Built in 1908 for a working-class choir, this concert hall is a celebration of Catalan identity.
  • Mercat de Santa Caterina · Sant Pere
    That wavy, colourful roof is made from over 300,000 ceramic tiles, in the colours of fruit and vegetables sold below.

  • Palau Güell · El Raval
    Gaudí built this mansion in the late 1880s for his patron, the industrialist Eusebi Güell, on a narrow street just off the Rambla.
  • Antic Hospital de la Santa Creu · El Raval
    For more than five hundred years this Gothic building was the city's main hospital, founded in 1401.
