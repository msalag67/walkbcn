Neighbourhood: El Raval

Arc del Teatre

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

Step under this arch off the Rambla and you leave the busy boulevard behind for the world of a novel. The Shadow of the Wind, by Carlos Ruiz Zafón, is one of the best-selling Spanish books ever, and it was written by a man born here in Barcelona. It came out in 2001 and became the first of four novels built around the same secret place. That place is the Cemetery of Forgotten Books, a vast hidden library where books the world has forgotten are kept safe. At the start of the story, in 1945, a young boy called Daniel Sempere is taken early one morning by his father, a bookseller, and led through these streets to a door in this very passage. Inside, he is allowed to choose one book and promise to protect it, and that choice sets the whole mystery in motion. Now walk a few steps in and let the lane close around you. Feel how quickly the noise of the Rambla fades, how the walls rise on either side, how the light narrows to a strip of sky above. It's easy to see why a writer would hide a secret here. The library itself is fictional, and no door in this lane leads to it. Still, readers from all over the world come to stand where you are and imagine which one it might be. As you look, remember that these doors belong to people's homes, so enjoy the story quietly and let the neighbours go about their day.

Tip: It's a narrow residential street: please don't knock on doors looking for the library.
