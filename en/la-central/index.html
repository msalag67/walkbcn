Neighbourhood: El Raval

La Central del Raval

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

You're standing on Carrer d'Elisabets, outside one of Barcelona's favourite independent bookshops, and it lives somewhere unexpected: inside a former chapel. The chapel once belonged to the Casa de la Misericòrdia, an old charitable institution of the Raval. Step to the doorway and let your eyes travel upward. The stone vaults still arch overhead, just as they did when this was a place of worship. But instead of an altar, there are tables piled with novels, art books and essays. Notice how the quiet of the old building suits its new use. People here tend to browse slowly, picking up a book, reading a few pages, putting it back, coming round again. The shop is part of La Central, a small group of independent Barcelona bookshops, and this branch is loved by locals as much as by visitors. Now look along the street. The Raval is full of buildings like this one, convents, chapels and charitable houses built over centuries, when this part of the old city was home to hospitals, monasteries and institutions caring for the poor. Just around the corner, the old Convent dels Àngels is now part of the MACBA, Barcelona's contemporary art museum, and the former Casa de la Caritat houses the CCCB cultural centre. Old religious buildings, reborn as places of culture: it's a story the neighbourhood tells again and again. If you go in, browse as the locals do, quietly and without hurry.

Tip: Browse quietly, as you would in a library, and support the shop if you find something you like.
