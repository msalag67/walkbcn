Neighbourhood: Poble-sec

Parc del Mirador del Poble-sec

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

This park hangs on the slope of Montjuïc, right above the dense streets of Poble-sec, a neighbourhood with little green space of its own. It is two parks in one. The upper part feels like a small forest, with wide gravel paths, stairs and viewpoints at the highest levels, among Aleppo pines. The lower part is an urban garden made for daily life: petanca courts, a playground, a ping-pong table, an area for dogs and a pergola covered in wisteria and Virginia creeper. Now follow the water, which ties the two parts together. A stream fed from a wall runs down an iron channel twenty-eight metres long into a small pond, and lower down another pond with water plants sends it over a stepped cascade eighteen metres high. Look at the trees too: jacarandas, which turn purple in late spring, Judas trees and Koelreuterias. Listen for a moment: the sounds here are neighbours playing petanca and children on the swings, not traffic. This is where Poble-sec comes to breathe, so enjoy it quietly.

Tip: It is the neighbourhood's park: share the paths with children and dog walkers, and keep music and voices low.
Enjoy it from outsideHomes all around: keep voices low
What to look at
The iron water channel, the ponds and the stepped cascade; the jacarandas and the wisteria pergola.
Where to listen
On a bench beside the upper pond, away from the playground.
Visiting
A free public park (Passeig de Montjuïc, 59), accessible for people with reduced mobility. The upper part is uphill with stairs.
