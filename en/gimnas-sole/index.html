Neighbourhood: El Raval

Gimnàs Solé

▶ Listen in WalkBCNFree audio story, no account

Nothing about this narrow street suggests history, yet FC Barcelona was born here. You're on Carrer de Montjuïc del Carme, in the Raval, and on 29 November 1899 a gymnasium on this street, the Gimnàs Solé, hosted a meeting that would change the city's sporting life. The man behind it was Joan Gamper, a Swiss football enthusiast who had been born Hans Gamper and settled in Barcelona. Football was still a fairly new game here, and he wanted a team. So he placed an advert in a sports newspaper, Los Deportes, asking for players. Those who answered came together in this gym that evening. They were a mixed group of Swiss, English and Catalan enthusiasts, united by a love of a game that many people in the city had barely seen. That night they founded the club. Take a moment to find the plaque that recalls the meeting. It's modest and easy to walk past, and that suits the story. There was no stadium, no crowd and no famous kit yet, just a handful of people in a small gym deciding to play together. Gamper stayed at the heart of the club for years, playing for it and later serving as its president more than once. Today his name lives on in the Joan Gamper Trophy, the pre-season match the club hosts every summer. Look along the street as it is now, with its balconies and front doors. People live here, so keep your voice low and leave doorways clear. From a small gym in the Raval to one of the most famous clubs in the world.

Tip: It's a residential street: look for the plaque quietly and don't block doorways.
